From-scratch multi-object tracking · a controlled study

When do the field's standard tricks actually help tracking?

An online tracking-by-detection system — an 8-state Kalman filter, an O(n³) Hungarian solver, and ByteTrack association — built from first principles on NumPy, then used as a reproducible, significance-tested study of when appearance re-ID, learned motion, and calibrated uncertainty pay off on real data (MOT17, DanceTrack). Several answers are honest negatives — which is the point.
