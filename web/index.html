Every object. Every frame. One stable ID.

VisionTrack follows every person, vehicle, or object across a video and holds a persistent identity for each — through crossings, occlusion, and clutter. A Kalman + Hungarian + ByteTrack core, built from scratch on NumPy, that runs on your own machine, on a file, or live in your browser. No cloud, no upload.

Where it's used

Turn a camera into counts, paths, and identities.

Persistent per-object identity is the primitive behind footfall, flow, and performance analytics. VisionTrack supplies that tracking layer; swap in whatever detector your domain needs — the identity logic stays the same.

Under the hood · a controlled research study

Not a wrapper — the tracker, built from first principles.

The core isn't a library call. It's an 8-state Kalman filter, an O(n³) Hungarian solver, and ByteTrack two-stage association, written from scratch on NumPy — then used as a reproducible, significance-tested study of when appearance re-ID, learned motion, and calibrated uncertainty actually help on real data (MOT17, DanceTrack). Several answers are honest negatives, which is the point.
