What happened to the two nearby trajectories?

This is a private research review, not the live tracker. Use the same 22 source frames in three passes. No software is being rerun and no conclusion is preselected.

Local, non-commercial MOTChallenge material · CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; additional rights may apply. Do not upload or redistribute these images. Notes stay in this tab unless you explicitly download them.

Start without boxes: step through the scene. Which movements are clear, and what is hard to distinguish? This pass does not use tracker IDs.

No overlays. Images are local files; this page makes no network requests.

Frame numbers are one-based. There is no interpolation, synthetic animation, automatic playback or face recognition.

Your observations, not an acceptance decision

A second person should review separately before reading the first person's interpretation. This page is a lightweight staged aid, not a secure blinded study. Opening it, checking hashes or downloading notes does not establish independent review or accurate labels.
